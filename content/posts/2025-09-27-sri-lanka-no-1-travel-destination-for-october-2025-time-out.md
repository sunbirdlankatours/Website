October marks the start of shoulder season in Sri Lanka, making it prime time to visit this gorgeous, sand-fringed island packed with culture for a snip. Though monsoon conditions can still affect the north, it’s still balmy and a good time to visit nature reserves like Yala National Park, surf the big waves of the east coast, and explore the incredible temples of the interior. Sri Lanka is a year-round destination, but heading there in the autumn while it’s a little less busy makes it feel even more of an oasis. Whether you’re hiking, exploring tea plantations, riding scenic railways or relaxing on palm-fringed beaches, there’s plenty to enjoy.

Why go now? The Hindu festival Diwali takes place in October every year and is celebrated in Sri Lanka with firecrackers, lights, singing, dancing and lavish meals. Visit a Hindu temple to light an oil lamp and seek a blessing from the Goddess Laksmi, or just enjoy the energy and colour the festival brings.

Time Out tip: Designers are flocking to the southern part of Sri Lanka, centred around Galle, to set up boutiques and new designer labels. Leave some room in your luggage if you like ceramics, natural gemstones and organic cotton clothes.

Average temp in Oct: 29C/84F
