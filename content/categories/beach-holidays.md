Sri Lanka has two beach seasons, because its two monsoons arrive at different times of year. The south and west coast, from Bentota to Galle and Mirissa, is at its best from December to April. The quieter east coast, around Trincomalee, Pasikudah and Arugam Bay, is at its best from April to September.

Every tour below mixes beach days with something more: tea hills, ancient cities or a safari. All of them are private and tailor-made, so tell us your dates and we will send you to the sunny side.
