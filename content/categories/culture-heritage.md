Sri Lanka has eight UNESCO World Heritage Sites, and most of them lie in the Cultural Triangle between Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa and Kandy. Climb Sigiriya, walk through the ruins of ancient capitals and visit the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy.

The tours below go further too: the Tamil north around Jaffna, Buddhist pilgrim routes, and village homes where old traditions are still part of daily life.
