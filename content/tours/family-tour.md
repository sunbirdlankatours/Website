This 10-day family tour is crafted to delight both kids and adults with a perfect blend of culture, nature, and relaxation. From climbing the iconic Sigiriya Rock Fortress and spotting elephants on safari to enjoying hands-on village experiences and scenic train rides, every day brings interactive adventures. Children will love the strawberry farms, turtle hatcheries, and beachside play, while parents can unwind with lush scenery, tea experiences, and colonial charm

## Itinerary

### Day 1 · Negombo · Relax and recover

- Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)
- Transfer to beach hotel in Negombo (short drive)
- Relaxing day: Pool time, beach walk, early dinner
- Optional: Small boat ride on the Dutch Canal
![](/img/tours/sunbird-lanka-airplane-runwayt.jpg) ![](/img/tours/dutch-canal-negombo.jpg)

### Day 2 · Negombo → Sigiriya · Lions and legends

- Drive to Sigiriya (approx. 3.5–4 hrs, with stops)
- Sigiriya Rock Fortress (kids love the lion paws and cave paintings)
- Optional: Climb halfway if with younger kids
- Check-in to a family-friendly eco-lodge with nature trails
![](/img/tours/sigiriya-01.jpg) ![](/img/tours/sigiriya-02.jpg)

### Day 3 · Minneriya · Elephants and village life

- Morning: safari at Minneriya or Kaudulla National Park – chance to see elephants!
- Afternoon: Village tour by bullock cart & canoe – see rural life, local food demo
- Enjoy a traditional lunch cooked by villagers
- Evening: Family games night at the hotel
![](/img/tours/safari-at-minneriya.jpg) ![](/img/tours/rice-and-curry.jpg)

### Day 4 · Sigiriya → Kandy · Sacred city and a cultural show

- Scenic drive to Kandy
- Stop at a spice garden (kids can touch and smell herbs)
- Temple of the Tooth Relic
- Stroll around Kandy Lake
- Evening: Watch a Kandyan cultural dance performance (drums, masks, fire!)
![](/img/tours/kandy-dance.jpg) ![](/img/tours/kandy-highlights.jpg)

### Day 5 · Kandy · Gardens, forests and museums

- Visit Udawatta Forest Sanctuary or Peradeniya Botanical Garden
- Afternoon: Visit the Ceylon Tea Museum or Gem Museum (short & kid-friendly)
- Optional: Puppet show or hands-on art workshop
![](/img/tours/peradeniya.jpg) ![](/img/tours/gems.jpg)

### Day 6 · Kandy → Nuwara Eliya · The train to tea country

- Morning scenic train ride from Kandy to Nanu Oya (book 1st class seats)
- Tea plantation & factory (short kid-friendly version)
- Gregory Lake – paddle boats, pony rides, bicycle rentals
- Stay in a colonial-style family lodge or resort
![](/img/tours/train-journey.jpg) ![](/img/tours/tea-factory.jpg)

### Day 7 · Nuwara Eliya · Strawberries and afternoon tea

- Optional visit to Horton Plains (early start for families with older kids)
- Play in Victoria Park (duck ponds, swings)
- Enjoy strawberry picking at a local farm
- Afternoon tea at Grand Hotel
![](/img/tours/horton-place.jpg) ![](/img/tours/nuwara-eliya.jpg)

### Day 8 · Nuwara Eliya → Bentota · Down to the beach

- Drive down to Bentota (approx. 5–6 hrs, scenic journey)
- Check into a beach resort with kid-friendly pool & play area
- Evening: Chill at the beach or resort
![](/img/tours/bentota-beach.jpg) ![](/img/tours/bentot-resort.jpg)

### Day 9 · Bentota · Water fun and turtle time

- Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery – see baby turtles!
- Optional boat safari on the Madu Ganga River (mangroves, monkeys, fish therapy)
- Watersports (banana boat, jet ski) – many are safe and family-friendly
- Leisure time by the sea
![](/img/tours/kosgoda-turtle-hatcherys.jpg) ![](/img/tours/banana-boat.jpg)

### Day 10 · Bentota → Colombo · City sights on the way home

- Transfer to Colombo (approx. 2 hours)
- Optional stops: Quick city tour (Galle Face Green, Independence Square)
- Optional stops:Shopping at Barefoot, ODEL, or local souvenir markets
- Transfer to airport for departure.
![](/img/tours/vihara-mahadevi-park.jpg) ![](/img/tours/return-to-colombo-and-departure.jpg)

## Journey highlights

- Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress – Ancient lion paws and panoramic views
- Wildlife Safari – Spot elephants in Minneriya or Kaudulla National Park
- Village Life Experience – Bullock cart rides, canoeing & traditional cooking
- Feel the rhythm of culture – Kandyan dance, drumming & fire-walking shows
- Kandyan Cultural Show – Drums, fire-walking & colorful costumes
- Scenic Train Ride – Through misty hills and tea plantations
- Strawberry Picking – Fun hands-on activity in Nuwara Eliya
- Turtle Hatchery Visit – Learn about sea turtle conservation in Kosgoda
- Beach Fun in Bentota – Kid-friendly resort with pool & watersports
- Art & Puppet Workshops – Creative time for young minds
- Botanical Gardens & Tea Tour – Nature walks and interactive learning

## Insightful tips

- Pace It Right: This itinerary balances activity and downtime—ideal for families with kids aged 4 and up.
- Pack Smart: Bring sun hats, insect repellent, swimwear, and comfy shoes for temple visits and light hikes.
- Choose Wisely: Many activities have flexible options for younger or older kids (e.g., halfway Sigiriya climb or full).
- Train Tickets: Book scenic train seats in advance—first-class offers great views and comfort for children.

## FAQ

### Is this tour suitable for toddlers?

Yes, with some adjustments. You can skip longer hikes and opt for more resort-based fun and shorter drives.

### Are child seats available during transport?

Yes, child seats can be arranged on request with most private drivers or tour companies.

### Are all accommodations family-friendly?

Absolutely! We recommend hotels and lodges with pools, play areas, and spacious family rooms.

### What food options are available for picky eaters?

Most hotels offer Western and kid-friendly meals like pasta, toast, fruit, and mild rice dishes.
