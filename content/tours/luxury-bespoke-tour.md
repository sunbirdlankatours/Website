Indulge in the ultimate Sri Lankan escape with our 10-Day Luxury Bespoke Tour — a seamless blend of culture, comfort, and curated experiences. From private helicopter rides over ancient ruins to sunset dinners on secluded beaches, every moment is tailored to you. Discover the island’s rich heritage, lush tea country, and golden shores in unmatched style and privacy.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 · Colombo · A private welcome

- VIP arrival assistance at Bandaranaike International Airport
- Private transfer to The Wallawwa or Shangri-La Colombo
- Afternoon: Spa session or short Colombo heritage walk with a private guide
- Dinner: Fine dining at Ministry of Crab or Kaema Sutra (curated Sri Lankan fusion)
![](/img/tours/sunbird-lanka-airplane-runwayt.jpg) ![](/img/tours/shangri-la-colombo-1.jpg)

### Day 2 · Colombo → Sigiriya · By helicopter to Lion Rock

- Optional: Scenic helicopter transfer to Sigiriya (or luxury SUV with stopovers)
- Check-in at Water Garden Sigiriya or Jetwing Vil Uyana
- Afternoon: Private guided tour of Sigiriya Rock Fortress
- Sunset cocktails with fortress views from your villa

### Day 3 · Dambulla & Minneriya · Culture and wilderness

- Visit Dambulla Cave Temple with an archaeologist guide
- Village experience in Hiriwadunna (exclusive lunch with locals in a curated setting)
- Private elephant safari at Minneriya or Eco Park in a luxury jeep
- Optional: Private dinner in the jungle with candlelight

### Day 4 · Polonnaruwa · The ancient city, or a spa day

- Customizable options: Private guided tour of Polonnaruwa ancient city by e-bikes

### Day 5 · Sigiriya → Kandy · The cultural heartbeat

- Luxury transfer to Kandy with a visit to a spice garden en route

### Day 6 · Kandy → Nuwara Eliya · First class to tea country

- Private car to Nanu Oya or scenic 1st class observation train ride through the mountains
- Tea plantation & factory (short kid-friendly version)
- Gregory Lake – paddle boats, pony rides, bicycle rentals
- Stay in a colonial-style family lodge or resort
![](/img/tours/luxury-tain-inside.jpg) ![](/img/tours/tea-trails.jpg)

### Day 7 · Nuwara Eliya · Slow days in the highlands

- Soft hiking trails or scenic helicopter charter over Horton Plains (optional)
- Picnic lunch in a tea estate or by a waterfall
- Spa, reading, croquet, and gourmet dining at your bungalow

## Insightful tips

- Best Time to Travel: November–April offers the most pleasant weather across the island.
- Dress Smart for Temples: Shoulders & knees covered for cultural visits – lightweight scarves work perfectly.
- Pack Essentials: Sunscreen, sunglasses, insect repellent, and light evening wear for resort dinners.
- Stay Connected: Most luxury hotels offer high-speed Wi-Fi and local SIMs can be arranged.
- Language & Hospitality: English is widely spoken. Your private chauffeur-guide ensures smooth communication everywhere.

## FAQ

### Can this tour be customized for honeymoons or anniversaries?

Absolutely! We offer romantic upgrades like sunset dinners, flower-petal turndowns, and curated couple’s experiences.

### Are helicopter transfers mandatory?

No – they are optional. All routes are available by luxury SUV with scenic stopovers.

### Is this tour suitable for families with children?

Yes – we can tailor the itinerary with kid-friendly luxury lodges, junior activities, and flexible pacing.

### How much luggage is allowed if we take helicopter transfers?

Light luggage (around 15kg per person in soft bags) is recommended. We can arrange for the rest to be transferred by road.

### Is a personal guide included throughout?

Yes – a licensed chauffeur-guide accompanies you throughout, and expert specialists join for key cultural experiences.
