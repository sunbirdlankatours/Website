Discover the charm of Negombo—one of Sri Lanka’s oldest fishing towns—on a hands-on fishing adventure in its scenic lagoon. Explore bustling fish markets, cruise through lush mangroves, and try traditional fishing techniques with local fishermen. Surrounded by coconut plantations and centuries-old canals, this tour offers more than just fishing—it’s a deep dive into culture, nature, and coastal life. Top it off with a relaxing catamaran ride for a truly unique Negombo experience!

![](/img/tours/negombo-lagoon-hilight-box01.jpg)

Discover both traditional and modern fishing methods in the heart of Negombo’s vibrant lagoon. Join local fishermen, try your hand at catching fresh seafood, and watch it come to life with a live cooking session—where your catch becomes a delicious, authentic Sri Lankan lunch.

![](/img/tours/negombo-lagoon-fishing-day-tour-animation.jpg)

Kick off your tour with a visit to the bustling Negombo Fish Market, where the day’s freshest catch awaits. Stroll through vibrant stalls, soak in the local atmosphere, and handpick your favorite seafood—soon to be transformed into a delicious lunch, just the way you like it.

## Tour highlights – not to miss!

- Enjoy a picnic lunch on a scenic island featuring freshly grilled seafood
- Choose your own fish at the local market for a truly personalized meal experience

## Things you'll experience

- Set off on a scenic boat tour of the Negombo Lagoon
- Take part in a live seafood cooking experience with your fresh catch
- Unwind on a catamaran ride through the lagoon, soaking in the stunning views

## FAQ

### Is this day tour right for me?

Absolutely! Whether you're a seafood lover, a nature enthusiast, or a culture explorer, discovering Negombo’s rich fishing heritage is an experience for everyone.

### Can the tour be customized?

Yes, the itinerary can be tailored to suit your preferences. Just let us know your interests in advance, and we’ll make it happen!

### When is the best time to go?

Negombo usually enjoys great weather for day tours year-round. However, between June and October, occasional rain from the southwest monsoon is possible—though tours still run during this period.

### What do the transfers include?

We provide hotel pickup, transport to all tour attractions, and a drop-off back at your hotel for a seamless experience.
