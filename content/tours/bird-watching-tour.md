Embark on a thrilling 15-day bird watching adventure across Sri Lanka’s diverse ecosystems, from misty cloud forests to coastal lagoons. This custom-designed itinerary takes you through world-renowned birding hotspots like Sinharaja Rainforest, Horton Plains, Bundala Wetlands, and Kalametiya Sanctuary—offering the chance to spot all 34 of Sri Lanka’s endemic bird species, alongside hundreds of migratory and resident birds. Whether it’s the vivid Sri Lanka Blue Magpie flitting through rainforest canopies, flamingos wading in salt flats, or the elusive Whistling Thrush singing in the highlands, every stop is a new encounter. Led by expert guides and staying in eco-friendly lodges close to nature, this journey is tailored for both avid birders and wildlife photographers seeking unforgettable sightings in one of the world’s top biodiversity hotspots.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 · Colombo · Wetland birds of the city

- Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)
- Accommodation: Cinnamon Red Colombo or Galle Face Hotel (comfort & convenience)
- Transport: Private airport transfer or taxi.
- Birding: Visit Thalawathugoda Wetlands or Bellanwila-Attidiya Sanctuary for resident and migratory waterbirds
![](/img/tours/sunbird-lanka-airplane-runwayt.jpg) ![](/img/tours/red-cinamon.jpg)

### Days 2–3 · Sinharaja · Rainforest endemics

- Transport: Private car or driver to Sinharaja (~5 hours from Colombo).
- Accommodation: Rainforest Eco Lodge or Blue Magpie Lodge (near Sinharaja).
- Bird Focus: Sri Lanka Blue Magpie (endemic & iconic) | Red-faced | Malkoha Spot-winged Thrush | Layard’s Parakeet | Sri Lanka Hanging Parrot
![](/img/tours/blue-magpie.jpg) ![](/img/tours/hanging-parrot.jpg)

### Days 4–5 · Kitulgala · Birds of the Kelani Valley

- Transport: Private car (2–3 hours from Sinharaja).
- Accommodation: Riverside Eco Lodges or Kitulgala Rest House.
- Bird Focus: Sri Lanka Dull-blue Flycatcher | Pied Thrush (seasonal) |Crested Serpent Eagle | Brown-backed Needletail (swifts) | Malabar Trogon
![](/img/tours/dull-blue-flycatche.jpg) ![](/img/tours/pied-thrush.jpg)

### Days 6–7 · Horton Plains · Highland specialists

- Transport: Drive to Horton Plains (~3 hours from Kitulgala).
- Accommodation: Horton Plains National Park Rest House or nearby Nuwara Eliya hotels (like The Grand Hotel).
- Bird Focus: Sri Lanka Whistling Thrush | Yellow-eared Bulbul | Sri Lanka Bush Warbler | Dull-blue Flycatcher | Black Eagle
![](/img/tours/yellow-eared-bulbul.jpg) ![](/img/tours/black-eagle.jpg)

### Days 8–9 · Nuwara Eliya · Hakgala Botanical Gardens

- Transport: Short drive (30 mins from Horton Plains).
- Accommodation: St. Andrew’s Hotel or Araliya Green Hills
- Bird Focus: Sri Lanka Scimitar Babbler | Spot-winged Thrush | Hill Swallow | Pied Thrush (seasonal visitor)
![](/img/tours/sri-lanka-scimitar-babbler.jpg) ![](/img/tours/hill-swallow.jpg)

### Days 10–11 · Udawalawe · Hornbills and storks

- Transport: Drive down (~4 hours from Nuwara Eliya).
- Accommodation: Elephant Trails Udawalawe or Grand Udawalawe Safari Resort.
- Bird Focus: Malabar Pied Hornbill | Sri Lanka Grey Hornbill | Painted Stork | Oriental Darter | Lesser Adjutant Stork
![](/img/tours/malabar-pied-hornbill.jpg) ![](/img/tours/oriental-darter.jpg)

### Days 12–13 · Bundala · Flamingos and waders

- Transport: 2-hour drive from Udawalawe to Bundala
- Accommodation: Cinnamon Wild Yala or nearby eco lodges in Kataragama.
- Bird Focus: Greater Flamingo | Asian Openbill Stork | Black-tailed Godwit | Crab-plover | Painted Stork
![](/img/tours/greater-flamingo.jpg) ![](/img/tours/painted-stork.jpg)

### Day 14 · Kalametiya · A quiet lagoon sanctuary

- Transport: 1.5-hour drive from Bundala.
- Accommodation: Local guesthouses or eco lodges near Kalametiya.
- Bird Focus: Black-headed Ibis | Brown-winged Kingfisher | Indian Pond Heron |Various shorebirds and waders.
![](/img/tours/brown-winged-kingfisher.jpg) ![](/img/tours/black-headed-ibis.jpg)

### Day 15 · Kalametiya → Colombo · Last birds, then home

- Transport: 3–4-hour drive back to Colombo for your flight.
- Optional final birding in Bellanwila-Attidiya Sanctuary or relax.
![](/img/tours/fliyng-bird.jpg) ![](/img/tours/beach-relax.jpg)

## Journey highlights

- Start in Colombo spotting waterbirds at urban wetlands
- Enter the emerald realm of Sinharaja, home to the dazzling Sri Lanka Blue Magpie and Red-faced Malkoha
- Explore the riverine forests of Kitulgala, tracking Malabar Trogons and swift-flying Needletails
- Climb to the cloud forests of Horton Plains for rare sightings of the Whistling Thrush and Yellow-eared Bulbul
- Visit Hakgala and Nuwara Eliya, a haven for highland endemics like the Scimitar Babbler and Pied Thrush
- Scan the open grasslands of Udawalawe for hornbills, storks, and raptors
- Marvel at migratory flocks in Bundala, including flamingos and godwits
- End by the coast in Kalametiya, where kingfishers, ibis, and waders gather

## Insightful tips

- Best season: December to April for migratory species and dry weather
- Pack essentials: Binoculars, bird guidebook/app, rain jacket, insect repellent, sun hat
- Photography tip: A 300mm+ telephoto lens helps capture distant or canopy-dwelling species
- Guiding: Use regional bird guides or full-time naturalists for maximum sightings

## FAQ

### How many bird species can I expect to see?

Over 200 species are possible, including all 34 endemics with an experienced guide.

### Is the tour physically demanding?

Moderate – most birding walks are slow-paced, but some forest trails and highland hikes may be uneven or steep.

### Can I tailor the trip to focus on certain bird families (e.g. raptors or endemics)?

Yes – this tour is fully customizable based on your target list and fitness level.

### Are lodges birder-friendly?

Absolutely – we choose accommodations near hotspots, many with in-house naturalists and early breakfast options.
