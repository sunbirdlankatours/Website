At Sunbird Tours, we use cookies to enhance your online experience, understand how our site is used, and deliver content tailored to your interests. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

### What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet, etc.) when you visit a website. They store bits of information to remember your preferences, improve functionality, and help us understand how users interact with our site. Each time you return, your browser sends these cookies back to our server—helping us provide you with a smoother, more personalized experience.

### How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

- Remember your browsing preferences
- Enhance site speed and performance
- Track website usage for analytics and improvements
- Enable social media sharing and embedded content
- Understand how users interact with our content and services

### Third-party cookies

Some features on our site may use third-party services such as Google Analytics, our live chat (Tawk.to), the language translator, and embedded maps or videos. These providers may place cookies on your device through our website.

We do not control the settings or behavior of these external cookies. We encourage you to check the respective privacy policies of these third-party providers for more information.

### Managing your cookie preferences

You have full control over cookies. Most web browsers allow you to:

- Accept or reject cookies automatically
- Remove previously stored cookies
- Receive alerts when cookies are being used

To adjust your settings, visit your browser’s “Help” section or see [www.allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org). Please note that disabling cookies may impact your ability to fully experience all features of our website.

### Questions?

We’re here to help. If you have any questions or concerns about our Cookie Policy, contact us at [letstravel@sunbirdtours.lk](mailto:letstravel@sunbirdtours.lk).
