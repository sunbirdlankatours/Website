## Sustainable Tourism Initiatives {#sustainability}

![](/img/site/sunbird-lanla-tours-sti.jpg) ![](/img/site/sunbird-lanla-tours-sti-highlightbox-eco-wild-life-03.jpg) ![](/img/site/sunbird-lanla-tours-sti-choose-better-01.jpg)

We are deeply committed to sustainable tourism that benefits both people and the planet. As guardians of Sri Lanka’s rich natural and cultural heritage, we strive to lead by example — minimizing our environmental footprint, promoting ethical travel experiences, and supporting local economies.

- **Low-impact tours** — Itineraries that limit group sizes, choose off-peak times, and avoid overcrowded destinations.
- **Green transportation** — Fuel-efficient and hybrid vehicles, with walking and cycling tours where possible.
- **Zero plastic commitment** — No single-use plastics in our operations; we encourage reusable bottles and eco-friendly bags.
- **Sustainable stays** — Eco-resorts, family-run guesthouses and green-certified hotels that employ local staff.
- **Wildlife & nature conservation** — We discourage unethical wildlife tourism such as elephant rides and animal shows, partner with ethical sanctuaries, and train our guides in Leave No Trace principles.
- **Supporting local & ethical trade** — Village tours, cooking classes, cooperatives and fair-trade artisan markets; fresh, organic meals from local farmers.
- **Carbon offset program** — Travelers can offset their trip’s emissions through reforestation and clean energy projects in Sri Lanka.
- **Educating for change** — Pre-travel sustainability tips for guests, responsible-tourism training for staff, and workshops with local communities.

*Sustainability isn’t a destination — it’s a way of traveling.*

## Corporate Social Responsibility {#csr}

![](/img/site/sunbird-lanka-tours-csr-env.jpg) ![](/img/site/sunbird-lanka-tours-csr-local.jpg) ![](/img/site/sunbird-lanka-tours-csr-edu.jpg)

We believe tourism is not just about seeing places — it’s about creating meaningful connections, uplifting communities, and preserving the environment for generations to come. As ambassadors of Sri Lanka’s beauty and heritage, we recognise our responsibility to protect and support the land and its people.

- **Environmental stewardship** — Supporting wildlife conservation projects, especially for elephants and marine life; reducing plastic use; partnering with eco-lodges and green-certified accommodations.
- **Community empowerment** — Working with local artisans, farmers and small businesses; creating jobs and fair wages through community-based tourism; village visits that respect cultural integrity.
- **Education & youth development** — School supply drives in rural areas, scholarships for students pursuing hospitality and tourism, and volunteering and internship opportunities for youth.
- **Health & wellbeing** — Sponsoring mobile medical camps in remote villages, mental wellness campaigns, and disaster relief when needed.

When you travel with Sunbird Lanka, you’re not just a tourist — you’re a partner in progress. *Leave only footprints, take only memories — and make a difference along the way.*
