# Main menu. Format:  Label | link | short note | photo
# Indent a line with two spaces to put it in the sheet that opens under the word above.
# In the sheet each line gets a number, its note in small letters and a photo that shows while you point at it.
# The photo is optional: without one, the line uses the photo of the page it links to.
# Two lines fill a sheet by themselves, so it never goes out of date:
#   @tour-categories   every tour category (content/categories), with "All N tours" under them
#   @regions           every destination region (content/destinations), with "All N places" under them
# A note on a top line (not indented) becomes the "see all" link under its sheet.
# An indented "button: Label | link" line puts a blue button at the foot of the sheet, before the "see all" link.
# Kept short on purpose: every other page is linked from the footer.
# Contact is in the About us sheet and behind the yellow "Plan my trip" button on every page.
