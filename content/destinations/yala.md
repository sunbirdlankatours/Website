Yala National Park is Sri Lanka’s top wildlife destination, famous for having one of the world’s highest leopard densities. Here, jungles meet the coastline, offering thrilling safaris with sightings of elephants, sloth bears, crocs, and peacocks. Beyond the wild, Yala hides ancient secrets—centuries-old stupas and reservoirs whisper stories of forgotten kingdoms.

**Best time to visit:** February to June is Yala’s dry season and has the most reliable wildlife sightings.

**Where to shop:** Yala is all about nature and adventure, so shopping options are minimal. Most hotels offer small boutiques with local handicrafts and souvenirs. For more variety, head to nearby towns for traditional items and artisan goods.

**Where to eat:** Dining in Yala blends luxury with wilderness charm. Many upscale hotels offer exceptional culinary experiences within their premises. For local flavors and casual dining, top picks include The Restaurant, The Chill, The Orchid, and Chef Lady Restaurant — each offering a unique taste of the region.

**Where to stay:** Kottawatta Village · Kottawatta River Bank Resort · Kele
