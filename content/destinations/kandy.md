Tucked in the central highlands, Kandy blends scenic charm with deep spirituality. Home to the sacred Temple of the Tooth and the vibrant Kandy Esala Perahera, this historic city pulses with Sinhala Buddhist heritage. Once the island’s final royal kingdom, Kandy is your gateway to Sri Lanka’s lush hill country and timeless traditions.

**Hidden gems**

- Bahirawakanda Buddha Statue – An 88ft-tall icon overlooking the city.
- Ceylon Tea Museum – Vintage brews and the story of Sri Lanka’s tea legacy.
- National Railway Museum – A nostalgic ride through locomotive history.
- Kandyan Dance & Drum Show – A vibrant cultural spectacle of rhythm and tradition.
- Spice Garden – Discover aromatic treasures in their natural setting.

**Best time to visit:** It has favourable conditions all year. November to April is Kandy’s dry season.

**Where to shop:** Kandy is a treasure trove for authentic Sri Lankan crafts. Stroll through the city centre to find vibrant handlooms at Selyn, intricate batiks at Jayamali Batik Studio, unique art pieces at Rangala House Gallery, and timeless finds at Waruna Antiques. It’s the perfect place to pick up meaningful souvenirs with cultural charm.

**Where to eat:** From traditional flavors to modern fusion, Kandy’s culinary scene has something for every palate. Enjoy gourmet dining at Theva Cuisine or Kandy House, savor local favorites at the iconic Kandy Muslim Hotel and White House, or unwind with great food and views at Rockview and Slightly Chilled Lounge. Each offers a unique taste of this historic city.

**Where to stay:** The Elephant Stables · Ellerton Bungalow · Aarunya Nature
