Welcome to Arugam Bay, the surf capital of Sri Lanka and one of the top 10 surf spots in the world. Tucked away on the island’s southeast coast, this laid-back beach town offers epic waves, golden sands, and a vibrant nightlife scene. Beyond surfing, explore secluded beaches, lush lagoons, nearby national parks, and a touch of hidden history. Whether you’re chasing waves or just the perfect sunset, Arugam Bay is your ultimate beach escape.

Nature, history & adventure—just beyond the waves!

**Hidden gems**

- Lahugala Park – Elephants & birdlife
- Magul Viharaya – Ancient royal site
- Okanda Devalaya – Sacred hilltop shrine
- Crocodile Point – Crocs by the lake
- Semanikulam Lake – Birds & buffalo views

**Best time to visit:** Surf season is from May to September. Suitable for off-season non-surf visits.

**Where to shop:** Whether you’re chasing waves or soaking up the sun, Arugam Bay has you covered with stylish swimwear, casual clothing, and surf gear. Stop by Apsara Bikini Tailor for custom beachwear, grab your board essentials at No Worries Surf Shop, or explore eco-friendly finds at Rice & Carry. It’s the perfect blend of local charm and coastal style.

**Where to eat:** From laid-back beach cafés to flavorful international cuisine, Arugam Bay serves up something for every taste. Top spots include Break Point and Hideaway Resort for chilled vibes, Why Not and the Roti Shop for quick bites, and Tandoori Hut for bold Indian flavors. For fresh, healthy options, check out Leaf and Vine, while Green Room, Gecko Café, and Beach Hut offer tasty meals with a view.

**Where to stay:** Jetwing Surf · Forest Rock · Bay Vista · Hideaway Resort
