Close to Habarana and the ancient cities, Minneriya is built around a great reservoir made by King Mahasen in the 3rd century. As the dry season goes on and the water draws back, fresh grass grows on its shores and elephants come from far around to feed, sometimes several hundred at once. This is “The Gathering”, one of the great wildlife sights of Asia.

More wild places nearby.

**Hidden gems**

- Kaudulla National Park – The herds often move here when Minneriya is quiet
- Hurulu Eco Park – Forest and grassland where elephants are seen early in the year
- Minneriya Tank – An ancient reservoir still watering the fields around it
- Birdlife – Painted storks, pelicans and eagles along the water’s edge

**Best time to visit:** July to October for The Gathering, when the most elephants come to the reservoir. Outside those months, guides often head for Kaudulla or Hurulu instead.
