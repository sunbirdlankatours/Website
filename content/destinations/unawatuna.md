Just a few minutes from Galle Fort, Unawatuna curves around a sheltered, palm-lined bay that is calm enough for swimming for much of the year. Beach cafés line the sand, turtles are often seen around the reef, and the forested headland of Rumassala hides one of the prettiest little beaches on the south coast.

Around the bay and over the hill.

**Hidden gems**

- Jungle Beach – A small, quiet cove below the forest on Rumassala
- Japanese Peace Pagoda – A white stupa on the hill with wide views over Galle
- Rumassala – In legend, a piece of the Himalayas dropped by Hanuman, rich in healing herbs
- Dalawella Beach – Rocks, rock pools and a famous rope swing a little further east

**Best time to visit:** December to April, when the south coast is dry and the sea is calm. The south-west monsoon brings rain and rougher water from May to September.
