Weligama, meaning “sandy village,” is a laid-back coastal gem on Sri Lanka’s southern shores. With its wide bay, perfect surf breaks, and iconic stilt fishermen, it’s a paradise for wave-chasers and culture lovers alike. Whether you're catching your first wave, lounging on untouched beaches, or indulging in fresh seafood, Weligama delivers the ultimate seaside escape—just minutes from Mirissa.

Beyond the Waves – Explore, Sip & Discover

**Hidden gems**

- Handunugoda Tea Estate – Home to rare White Virgin Tea and 25 specialty lowland brews.
- Ahangama Beach – A serene blend of surfable waves, sandy stretches, and rocky charm.
- Martin Wickramasinghe Folk Museum – A cultural time capsule of Sri Lanka’s literary legend.
- WaterMelon Surf Club – Learn to surf or gear up with friendly locals and quality rentals.
- Dewundara Point – Whale and dolphin sightings await off this stunning southernmost cape.

**Best time to visit:** Weligama is a year-round destination.

**Where to shop:** Shopping in Weligama is low-key but charming. The Ceylon Sliders shop offers trendy clothing, accessories, and travel essentials. Along the roadside, you’ll find traditional lacework stalls, perfect for picking up locally made crafts. For broader shopping, Galle is just a short drive away.

**Where to eat:** Weligama is a food lover’s haven with a mix of laid-back beach vibes and flavorful cuisine. From fresh seafood at Fish Point and elevated dining at W15, to casual bites at Rotti Shop and Hangten Rooftop Restaurant, there’s something for every palate. Don’t miss the sweet treats at AVM Cream House, or the cozy charm of Kaori Café, Soul Café, Aloha Home and Kitchen, and Froggy — each adding their own unique flavour to the Weligama food scene.

**Where to stay:** Bay Marriott · Cape · Malabar Hill
