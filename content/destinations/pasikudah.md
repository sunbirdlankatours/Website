Pasikudah is a wide, sheltered bay on the east coast where a reef far offshore keeps the sea calm and shallow. You can walk out a long way with the water only at your waist, which makes it one of the gentlest beaches on the island for swimming, for children and for simply floating in the sun.

More of the quiet east coast.

**Hidden gems**

- Kalkudah Beach – The longer, emptier bay just next door
- Batticaloa Fort – A Portuguese and Dutch fort on the lagoon, about an hour to the south
- Batticaloa Lagoon – Known for its “singing fish”, heard on still full-moon nights
- Snorkelling – The reef off Pasikudah shelters fish close to shore

**Best time to visit:** April to September, when the east coast is dry and the bay is at its calmest. The north-east monsoon brings rain from November to January.
