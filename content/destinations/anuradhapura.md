Discover Sri Lanka’s first capital and the sacred heart of its heritage. From ancient Buddhist temples to massive stupas and the world’s oldest sacred tree, Anuradhapura blends history, spirituality, and stunning ruins. A UNESCO World Heritage Site and part of the Cultural Triangle, this city is a must-visit for culture lovers and curious travelers alike.

Secrets Worth Discovering

**Hidden gems**

- Sri Lanka’s most intricate ancient stone carving.
- Rare relics from Anuradhapura’s golden era.
- The finest guard stone of the ancient world.
- Ingenious ancient bathing pools.
- A serene statue of perfect meditation.

**Best time to visit:** It is a year-round destination. March to July is the hottest season. June is the busiest month.

**Where to shop:** Near the ancient sites, you’ll find small vendors selling Buddha statues, souvenirs, fresh fruit, corn on the cob, and other local treats—perfect for a quick bite or a keepsake. In town, you’ll have access to pharmacies, general stores, and banks, making it easy to stock up on essentials during your visit.

**Where to eat:** While Anuradhapura isn’t known for fine dining outside of hotels, you’ll still find some great local gems. Try Sanctuary at Tissawewa for a serene, heritage-inspired meal, enjoy casual bites at LuLu’s Feeding Point, relax by the water at The Lakeside, or grab something cozy and tasty at Banana Cafe.
