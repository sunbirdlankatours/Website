Just east of Anuradhapura, the rocky hill of Mihintale is where the monk Mahinda is said to have met King Devanampiya Tissa and brought Buddhism to Sri Lanka in the 3rd century BC. A long stone stairway climbs past ancient ruins to white stupas and a summit rock with views across the forests and lakes of the north.

A climb through history.

**Hidden gems**

- Aradhana Gala – The summit rock where Mahinda is said to have first spoken to the king
- Naga Pokuna – A pool cut into the rock, with a five-headed cobra carved beside it
- Kantaka Cetiya – One of the oldest stupas on the island, with fine carved panels
- Poson – Every June, pilgrims dressed in white fill the hill for the Poson full moon
