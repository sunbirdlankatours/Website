Udawalawe is one of the best places in Asia to see wild elephants. Herds graze across open grassland around the Udawalawe reservoir, often close to the jeep tracks, alongside water buffalo, crocodiles, deer and a great variety of birds. Its open landscape makes animals easy to spot at any time of year.

Beyond the jeep tracks.

**Hidden gems**

- Elephant Transit Home – Orphaned elephant calves are fed here before being returned to the wild
- Udawalawe Reservoir – Water birds and wide views over the dam at sunset
- Birdlife – Eagles, bee-eaters and peacocks are seen on almost every drive
- Early starts – Morning and late-afternoon safaris catch the animals at their most active
