Often hailed as the Eighth Wonder of the World, Sigiriya is a 200-metre-high ancient rock fortress rising from Sri Lanka’s jungle heartland. Once a royal palace built by King Kashyapa in the 5th century, it dazzles with landscaped gardens, mirror walls, and legendary lion paws guarding the entrance. Climb to the summit for sweeping views and a walk through history at Sri Lanka’s most iconic UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Off the Beaten Path – Discover History, Hikes & Hidden Art

**Hidden gems**

- Pidurangala Rock – A rewarding climb with stunning views of Sigiriya.
- Pidurangala Vihara – A serene ancient temple nestled at the base of the rock.
- Cobra Hood Cave – A natural rock formation shaped like a cobra’s hood.
- Dakinigiriya Vihara – Tranquil ruins by a lake, perfect for peaceful exploration.
- Sigiri Art Gallery – A vibrant stop for local art, masks, and handwoven textiles.

**Best time to visit:** October to December is Sigiriya’s wettest season. Early morning or late afternoon climbs are the best.

**Where to shop:** For a taste of local craftsmanship, head to Pathikada or the Sigiriya Craft Village for handmade treasures. Lanka Silks offers elegant textiles, while Vishwa Gems is the place to shop for Sri Lankan gemstones and jewellery.

**Where to eat:** Sigiriya serves up a rich mix of dining experiences. Enjoy fine dining at Heritance Kandalama, Water Garden, or Jetwing Vil Uyana, or savour authentic Sri Lankan dishes at popular local spots like Gamagedara, Ahinsa, Chooti, Nirvana, and Rasta Rant.

**Where to stay:** Water Garden · Jetwing Vil Uyana · Wild Grass
