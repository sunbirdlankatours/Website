Adam’s Peak, or Sri Pada, rises to 2,243 metres above the tea country of the central highlands. At the summit is a sacred footprint, honoured by Buddhists, Hindus, Muslims and Christians alike. Pilgrims climb through the night by a lit stairway of several thousand steps to reach the top in time for sunrise.

Before and after the climb.

**Hidden gems**

- The peak’s shadow – At sunrise a perfect triangle of shadow falls across the hills to the west
- Dalhousie – The small village at the foot of the main path, where the climb begins
- Tea houses on the way – Small stalls with hot tea and a place to rest all the way up
- Little Adam’s Peak – An easy walk near Ella with a similar view, for a gentler day

**Best time to visit:** The pilgrimage season runs from December to May, when the path is lit and the sky is clearest. Most people start around 2 a.m. from Dalhousie to reach the summit for sunrise.
