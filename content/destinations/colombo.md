Most journeys across Sri Lanka begin and end in Colombo, and the city rewards a day of its own. Old colonial streets sit beside glass towers, Buddhist temples beside busy bazaars, and every evening the whole city seems to gather on the seafront at Galle Face Green as the sun drops into the Indian Ocean.

See the city between the airport and the island.

**Hidden gems**

- Jami Ul-Alfar Mosque – Pettah’s red-and-white “Red Mosque”, finished in 1909
- Wolvendaal Church – A Dutch church from 1749, among the oldest Protestant churches in the country
- Seema Malaka – Geoffrey Bawa’s floating temple on Beira Lake
- Pettah markets – Narrow lanes of spices, fabrics and fruit, loud and full of life

**Best time to visit:** December to March is the driest time on the west coast. From May to September the south-west monsoon brings afternoon showers, but the city stays easy to explore.
