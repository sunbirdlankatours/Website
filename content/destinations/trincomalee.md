On the north-east coast, Trincomalee wraps around one of the finest natural harbours in the world. The Koneswaram temple stands high on Swami Rock above the sea, the beaches of Uppuveli and Nilaveli stretch north of town, and a short boat ride away the reefs of Pigeon Island are full of colourful fish and reef sharks.

Beyond the beaches of the east.

**Hidden gems**

- Kanniya Hot Springs – Seven warm wells, each a slightly different temperature
- Fort Frederick – The old fort on the way up to Koneswaram, where spotted deer wander between the walls
- Marble Beach – A quiet bay of clear, calm water south of the harbour
- Whale watching – Blue whales and dolphins pass offshore from about March to August

**Best time to visit:** April to September, when the east coast is at its sunniest and the sea is calm. From November to January the north-east monsoon brings rain.

**Where to stay:** Jungle Beach by Uga Escapes
