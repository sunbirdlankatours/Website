On the dry south-east coast, Bundala is a quiet park of shallow lagoons, scrub and sand dunes. It was Sri Lanka’s first wetland protected under the Ramsar Convention, and in the cooler months it fills with migrant birds, among them flocks of greater flamingos. Crocodiles bask along the water, and sea turtles come ashore on its beaches.

A birder’s park on the south coast.

**Hidden gems**

- Greater flamingos – Seen on the lagoons in the migrant season
- Crocodiles – Mugger crocodiles rest along the banks of the lagoons
- Turtle beaches – Several kinds of sea turtle nest along the shore
- A quieter safari – Fewer jeeps than nearby Yala, often with elephants too

**Best time to visit:** September to March, when the migrant birds and flamingos arrive. The park is open all year, and the early morning is the best time to see birds.
