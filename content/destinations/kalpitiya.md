Kalpitiya is a long, narrow peninsula on the north-west coast, with the open ocean on one side and a sheltered lagoon on the other. Large pods of spinner dolphins feed offshore, steady winds have made the lagoon a favourite of kitesurfers, and small mangrove islands dot the water of Dutch Bay.

Life between the lagoon and the sea.

**Hidden gems**

- Bar Reef Marine Sanctuary – One of the largest coral reefs around Sri Lanka
- Kalpitiya Dutch Fort – A small coral-stone fort from the 1600s
- St. Peter’s Church – An old Dutch church in Kalpitiya town
- Mangrove islands – Quiet boat trips through the channels of Dutch Bay

**Best time to visit:** November to April for dolphin watching on calm seas; May to October for the strongest winds and the best kitesurfing.
